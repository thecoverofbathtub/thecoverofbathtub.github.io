8. Tradeoffs

Strategy & Executiontradeoff

Ads is probably one of the most scrutinized and measurable industries. Lots of tradeoffs:

  • Recommendation system’s latency → lost revenue + better recommendation quality
  • Increase ad load → damaging long-term organic engagement → affecting long-term revenue
  • Increase clickable area → volume of clicks vs. quality
  • Volume: better attribution credit, but degrade ranking quality + organic engagement
  • Quality: loss credit, users convert on the spot
  • Early-stage dev velocity vs. better architecture → don’t introduce unnecessary complexity, refactor when needed

Methodology

  • Zoom-in to find opportunities, e.g. EXB MENA
  • Think deeply & thoroughly to exhaust all options → try to achieve both, e.g. offline & online
  • Build functional product levers + clearly understand its impact → decisions change over time
